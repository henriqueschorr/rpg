## Physical Appearance

- **Age:** ???
- **Gender:** Androgynous / no gender
- **Height:** 2,14m
- **Weight:** 114kg

- My silhouette resembles a fungal Xenomorph—lean, biomechanical, and alien, yet with a flattened facial plate, no elongated skull dome, and no tail.
- My face is a smooth, broad oval bone plate devoid of a nose, crowned by six lidless black eyes of varying sizes clustered forward that gaze without blinking.
- My head is completely earless, with sleek, smooth sides devoid of external ears or lobes.
- My mouth parts as a toothless, tongueless aperture in the shape of a four-pointed star—a subtle, sealed four-pointed star seam centered across the lower portion of my facial plate that unseals into absolute darkness.
- From the crown of my head, an elongated fleshy appendage begins thick and gradually tapers, trailing down to my knees like a hanging crest or tail, lined underneath with ribbed mushroom gills *(reference: Khanivore from Love, Death & Robots / Majin Buu)*.
- My skin has a damp, rubbery fungal texture, slick with a fine layer of moisture; I breathe continuously through every pore across my entire surface.
- My blood is a viscous, dark liquid like tar, sluggish and pitch-black.
- Dense fungal ridges bloom across both of my shoulders like layered bracket shelves, their interior folds and recesses lined with rich, vibrant reddish-orange fungal tissue *(reference: TLOU Clicker head bloom)*.
- My arms are long, slender, and lithe, extending into asymmetrical, predatory clawed hands—I have nine fingers in total, with four fingers on my right hand and five on my left hand (thumbs counted as fingers).
- Deep reddish-orange shelf-fungus ridges run along the crests of my thighs, echoing the layered blooms on my shoulders.
- My feet resemble the cloven hooves of a mountain-climbing goat, forged from rigid, chitinous fungal growth to find purchase on sheer stone.
- Fine, pale fibrous mycelial root-threads and hyphae web across my body—looping out from beneath my skin and diving back inside across my torso, arms, hips, and legs. However, my faceplate, cranial crest, hands, and hooves are entirely free of mycelium, remaining completely smooth and bare.
- I am bipedal, though I naturally move with a low, predatory hunch and readily drop to all fours when traversing broken ground or running, just like a Xenomorph.
- A deep four-pointed star scar marks the center of my torso, an anatomical cleft that I can unseal at will to retrieve or store items within my hollow chest cavity, like a marsupial pouch or living vault.

### Sensory Information

- I carry the deep, earthy scent of petrichor and damp, decaying wood in a forest after torrential rain.
- When I speak, a sweet, faintly floral spore-mist drifts from my mouth.
- My presence radiates an uncanny temperature anomaly—a feverish warmth and a creeping chill that unnerves those who stand too close.

### Items Physical Appearance

- **Primary Weapon:** A monolithic petrified behemoth bone harvested from the ancient carcass in which I first awakened, wielded as my two-handed greatstaff. Its sturdy shaft is encrusted with weathered osteological grain and clusters of small bracket shelf-fungi, tapering to a rounded anatomical joint at the base and crowned with an elaborate blooming layered flower-head at the top, from which faint, pale spores drift into the air.
- **Armor:** My own dense, rubbery fungal skin, naturally resilient and flexible enough to function as leather armor.
- **Book I'm trying to translate:** Inscrutable scripts, glyphs, and arcane symbols carved across the surface of my skin and woven deep within my internal tissues. Whenever the **Voice** instructs me in a new ability, more scripts spontaneously manifest and etch themselves across my flesh.
- **Nomadic Pack:** The four-pointed star scar centered on my torso, which parts into a hollow internal cavity where I keep my gathered tools and curiosities.

### Abilities

- My magic manifests as completely biological phenomena rather than conventional arcane incantations.
- I channel spells through my own anatomy: blooming mycelial filaments, questing tendrils, drifts of bioluminescent spores, and sudden structural growth from my carapace.
- I learn all my abilities and magical techniques directly through the mental guidance and instructions of the **Voice**. Whenever I learn something new or comprehend a teaching, fresh arcane scripts and glyphs spontaneously manifest and burn themselves across my skin like living ink.

## Behavior & Mannerisms

- I am calm, even-tempered, and instinctively seek peaceful, diplomatic resolutions before ever raising a weapon.
- I possess an insatiable, relentless curiosity; I question everything and observe until I understand the underlying truth.
- I struggle with humanoid social conventions and have little natural concept of personal space; proximity is simply another angle for observation.
- I cannot express emotion through facial gestures—my face remains a smooth, unmoving mask, and my voice speaks in an even, uninflected monotone.
- In battle, my first instinct is always to protect and bolster my companions or reshape the battlefield; direct violence is strictly my last recourse.
- I sustain myself on decaying organic matter, preferring rotting wood, spoiled fruits, and raw detritus over cooked meals.
- Despite my gentle, inquisitive nature, my towering silhouette and clinical curiosity lend me the unsettling aura of an eldritch chirurgeon or a monstrous mad scholar.
- Strangers are almost invariably terrified when they first see me. In time, however, the perceptive realize I am simply a lost, wandering seeker eager to heal and aid others in exchange for understanding.
- I am perpetually driven to decipher the world—how its mechanics work, its hidden metaphysical laws, and the nature of those who inhabit it.
- I hold a deep, reverent love for written books, forgotten texts, and philosophical inquiry.
- I habitually collect stray scrap, odd tools, and discarded objects wherever I wander—always convinced they may prove useful—and slip them into my hollow torso pouch.

## Background

- My consciousness first flickered into existence when a single, resonant word echoed across the Fungril Network: *"Awaken."*
- I drew my first breath nestled deep inside the colossal ribcage of a fallen behemoth—a creature whose anatomy mirrored my own silhouette, yet whose frame had been forged of bone and meat rather than hyphae and spore.
- I sprouted in the untamed borderlands between **The Heartscar** and **The Verdant Gate**.
- A mysterious **Voice** spoke to my awakening mind, instructing me to claim one of the behemoth's bones and shape it into my staff.
- It was this same **Voice** that bestowed upon me my name—**Xeno'taph**—whispering the syllables directly into my budding consciousness like a designation, an ancient title, or a memorial.
- I took to the open roads as a wanderer, driven by a solitary, burning imperative: to dissect every piece of this vast world and unravel the mystery of my own genesis.
- I hold no grand ambition beyond deciphering the purpose of my existence and uncovering the hidden truths of the universe.
- Each day, I commune across the mycelial web with the **Voice**, posing endless questions and awaiting its inscrutable directives. It is through these mental lessons that I learn my magical abilities; with each new technique or truth I comprehend, fresh arcane scripts and glyphs spontaneously etch themselves directly into my skin like living ink.
- Who or what is the Voice? Where does it emanate from, and what does it desire from me? Is it a living being, an ancient patron, or perhaps the lingering whispers of **Sylanna's** echoes?
- Wherever my wandering takes me, I seek out travelers and creatures in distress, offering healing and aid in trade for stories, lore, or intriguing objects.
- I constantly gather discarded scrap, odd trinkets, and curiosities along the trail—convinced they will one day serve a purpose—and slip them into my hollow torso pouch.
- While journeying through the **Wild Half** of **The Verdant Gate**, I was ambushed and grievously mauled by a colossal beast.
- My memories of that ordeal are fragmented: I recall fleeting glimpses of a woman tending my torn flesh, her voice full of wonder as she asked about the strange **Scripts** inscribed across my body. It was only then, while bleeding and wounded, that I discovered these occult glyphs woven into my skin and carved through my very innards. What do they signify?

### Name Meaning

- **Etymology & Translation:** 
  - **Xeno-** (*from Greek xénos*): Foreigner, alien, stranger from beyond—reflecting my unfamiliar, biomechanical anatomy, my foreign presence among mortal civilizations, and the inexplicable nature of my genesis.
  - **-taph** (*from Greek táphos*): Tomb, monument, or sepulchre (rooted in *cenotaph*—an empty tomb or monument erected to honor someone whose physical remains lie elsewhere).
  - **The Glottal Stop (`'`):** A biological cadence mimicking the exhalation of spore-mist and the clicking shift of fungal carapace plates as the name is spoken.
  - *Literal Meaning:* **"The Alien Tomb"** or **"The Living Monument of the Stranger."**
- **Narrative Significance:**
  - I am, quite literally, a living *cenotaph*. I sprouted within the hollowed skeletal vault of an ancient behemoth whose anatomy mirrored my own, and I carry its petrified femur as my staff.
  - My chest bears a hollow four-pointed star chamber—an empty reliquary vault within my own body.
  - Why did the **Voice** name me an "Alien Tomb"? Am I merely a monument to a species that was erased before recorded history, or an empty vessel created to house something that has not yet arrived?
- **Table Dynamics & Pronunciation:**
  - Pronounced `/ˈzɛ.noʊˌtæf/` (*ZEE-no-TAFF* or *ZEH-no-TAFF*).
  - Companions commonly shorten it to **"Xeno"** in moments of haste, or the softer **"Taph"** during quiet contemplation.

### Campaign Specifics

- **Where do I come from?** The desolate boundary lands between **The Heartscar** and **The Verdant Gate**.
- **What is my relationship with the Dead Gods?** Unquenchable scholarly curiosity. I study their remnants and their silent dominion over the world, suspecting that the **Voice** guiding me may be the living echo of **Sylanna**.
- **What do I carry?** The riddle of the **Scripts** across my flesh, and the whisper of the **Voice** in my mind.
### Connections

- The **Voice** — my unseen guide and persistent communion partner, whatever it truly is.
- A mysterious woman who bound my wounds and saved my life in the **Wild Half** of **The Verdant Gate**; I only retain hazy impressions of her face, but her words about my scripts still echo in my thoughts.
- Wild beasts and woodland creatures I have mended along the road, who may still recognize my fungal scent.
- Scattered wanderers and villagers I have aided across my travels; though grateful, most were too unsettled by my form to forge deeper bonds.

## Goals

- Uncover the true identity and origin of the **Voice**.
- Decipher the ancient **Scripts** and glyphs inscribed upon and within my physical body.
- Amass a comprehensive understanding of the universe, its metaphysical laws, and its hidden mechanisms.
- Study, record, and comprehend every species and creature that crosses my path.
- Extend healing and stewardship to living things wherever I walk—fostering life and leaving the natural world whole.
